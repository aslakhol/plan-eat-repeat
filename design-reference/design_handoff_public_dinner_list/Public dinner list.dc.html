Spaghetti Carbonara

QuickComfortPastaItalian4 servings

Ingredients

400 gspaghetti
150 gpancetta — diced
4 pcseggs
100 gpecorino — finely grated
1 tspblack pepper

Method

1Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente.
2Fry the pancetta until crisp while the pasta cooks.
3Beat the eggs with the pecorino and plenty of pepper.
4Off the heat, toss the drained pasta with the pancetta, then the egg mixture, loosening with pasta water until glossy.
